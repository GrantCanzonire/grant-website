Cybersecurity Awareness Project

The Cybersecurity Awareness Project is an educational resource currently in development aimed at helping everyday users understand and apply basic cybersecurity practices. This initiative bridges my personal interest in cybersecurity with my Information Systems & Analytics studies at Louisiana State University, focusing on making critical security principles accessible and relevant.

Planned Topics

  • Recognizing phishing attempts
  • Creating strong passwords
  • Multi-factor authentication
  • Protecting personal information online
  • General online safety

Project Goals

  • Make cybersecurity concepts easier to understand for everyday users
  • Develop my personal knowledge and foundation in cybersecurity
  • Create a useful educational resource that encourages safer digital habits
